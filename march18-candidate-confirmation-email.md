**YOUR SPEAKING SLOT**

Speaking slots are brief by design. You'll have **2 minutes** — timed, with a 30-second warning — to introduce yourself and answer one of the following questions. Your choice:

1. What is the single most important action you will take in your first 90 days in office to advance statehood or defend home rule?
2. What's your response to a constituent who says statehood is a lost cause under the current Congress — why should they keep fighting?
3. What is one specific thing DC residents can do right now — beyond voting — to advance statehood?

After all candidates have spoken, we'll open the floor for **moderated Q&A** from attendees via the Zoom chat.
