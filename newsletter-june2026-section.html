2026 DC Primary: Who Answered the Questionnaire?

Before the June 16 primary, RepresentDC sent our 7-question statehood questionnaire to every Democratic candidate for public office and every candidate for DC Democratic Party committee. Here's who responded — and what they said.

61.5%

Mayor

5 of 8 responded — 62.5%

Janeese Lewis George

"Several times over the last two years, I've also walked the halls of Capitol Hill, meeting with members of Congress to build a coalition to defend DC and promote Statehood. Many Congress members have told me I am the first DC elected official who's ever met with them. That's a real shame. I worked with unions to help broker a meeting with a Democratic member of Congress who wasn't voting with DC. I was able to persuade him of the impact those votes have on the people of DC and I am pleased to share that the Congress member I met with now consistently votes to support DC and protect our autonomy."

Gary Goodweather
