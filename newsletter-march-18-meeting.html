DC Democrats Statehood Committee

Candidates Sound Off on Statehood

March 18, 2026 Meeting Recap

On March 18th, the DC Democrats Statehood Committee held a special candidate forum — and 15 candidates running for DC offices in 2026 showed up to go on the record about statehood and home rule.

Each candidate gave a 2-minute statement responding to one of three questions:

  1. What is the single most important action you will take in your first 90 days in office to advance statehood or defend home rule?
  2. What's your response to a constituent who says statehood is a lost cause under the current Congress?
  3. What is one specific thing DC residents can do right now — beyond voting — to advance statehood?

Below is a summary of who spoke, what they said, and links to their full questionnaire responses on the DC Candidate Statehood Tracker.

State of Statehood — Context

The meeting opened with urgent updates: the House Oversight Committee passed a bill (21–19, party-line) overturning two DC laws — our automated traffic enforcement program and our right-turn-on-red ban. Every Democrat in both chambers voted to support DC.

The Fair Budget Coalition also previewed a new national solidarity campaign launching in May: Together for DC, aimed at building grassroots support across the country for DC autonomy and statehood.
