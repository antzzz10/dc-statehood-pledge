DC is under attack.
We asked every candidate: will you fight back?

Congress has introduced 74+ bills targeting DC — blocking $1.1 billion of our budget, overturning local laws, and stripping away home rule. The people who win in June will decide how DC responds.

So we asked. Every declared candidate for DC elected office and DC Democratic State Committee received a 7-question statehood questionnaire. 205 candidates. Every party. Every race.

25 have responded so far. 100% support statehood.

Here’s some of what they said:

“Let’s be clear: if the Republicans thought THEY would get 2 new Senators from making DC a state, we’d have been a state for years. We have to call out this hypocrisy — and at the same time, find avenues beyond shame (because they have so little).”

— Kinney Zalesne, candidate for Delegate to Congress

“I am deeply alarmed by what we are witnessing. We are watching democratic norms erode in real time. We are seeing the will of voters undermined, local decision-making attacked, and power consolidated in ways that silence communities. And I’m angry.”

— Rashida Brown, candidate for Ward 1 Council

“Statehood must be framed not as a local preference, but as unfinished civil rights work.”

— Markus Batchelor, candidate for Shadow Senator

— Philip Pannell, candidate for National Committeeman (Free DC Slate)

“As soon as Congress introduces a bill that solely affects our local laws or money, then every member of Congress becomes our member of Congress.”

— Michelle Chappell, candidate for Ward 1 Committeewoman (Free DC Slate)

“DC statehood will not be gifted from above — it will be won by the people who live here.”

— Slobodan Milic, candidate for Ward 4 Committeeman (Free DC Slate)

Here’s what you can do right now.

180 candidates still haven’t gone on the record. This June’s primary is a rare chance to get a large group of current and future DC leaders to commit — publicly — to defending our right to govern ourselves. That only happens if voters demand it.

At every candidate forum this spring, ask: “What specifically will you do to defend home rule and advance statehood?” Make them answer. Every time.

Know a candidate who hasn’t responded? Send them the tracker. The questionnaire takes 10 minutes and is still open. No one gets to sit this one out.

Share the tracker with your neighbors, your ANC listserv, your group chats. The more voters who see it, the harder it is for candidates to stay silent.
