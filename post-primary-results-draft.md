# Post-Primary Archive: Results & Banner Copy (Draft)

Status: data + copy approved 2026-06-21. As of 2026-07-13, both previously-pending items are resolved — Ward 1 certified (Raj won outright, RCV round 4, 52%); DC Dem Party Committee slate outcome known (Free DC 36/48 seats), formal DCBOE certification lands 2026-07-17 but doesn't block anything since that race is stats-only/out of accountability scope. Nothing blocks implementation now. Scope: accountability quotes/results limited to public office races (not the 48-seat Dem Party Committee).

## Banner copy (approved)

> **Congratulations to DC's 2026 primary winners — and the real work starts now.**
>
> Every election cycle, RepresentDC asks candidates one thing: will you make DC statehood non-negotiable? Not a talking point, not a someday issue — a first-order priority, every term, until it's done.
>
> Below is how this June's primary winners answered. Some made statehood central to their campaigns. Some didn't answer at all. We're publishing this not to score points, but because the people who will hold these offices for the next four years made commitments — or didn't — and DC residents deserve a record to hold them to.
>
> *Note: DC's June primary effectively decides most of these races in advance of November's general election.*
